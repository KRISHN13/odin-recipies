2-Min Vali Maggie


This is a recipie of my favourate way to make the ready to cook maggie at home by urself Try it and Enjoy!

Ingredients Needed

  • A pack of Elbow Macaroni pasta
  • Red Chillie Sauce
  • Soy Sauce
  • Home Spices(salts,chillie powder,ect)
  • Some Veggies like bell pepper,tomato,onion,ect.
  • Garlic

Steps to follow

  1. Add Double the water needed to fully drench the amount of pasta added and 1 table spoon of groundnut oil for about 500ml of water so change it according to the amount of water added.
  2. Let the pasta boil in the water until they are double the side and soft and squishy.
  3. Strain out all the water and keep the pasta out to dry until then get a put added 2 tablespoon of oil in it.
  4. added few spices in the oil with the veggies of your choice and then let the veggitables sizzle till cooked.
  5. after the veggies are cooked ass the chillie sauce and soy sauce and right after that add the pasta in the pot.
  6. Let the pasta and the gravey cook together for 5 min and then turn of the flame and the pasta is ready to serve hot.
